I was surprised when our workshop paper was announced as a **Best Paper Award Finalist**, and thrilled to present our work in the workshop session. The feedback I received during my poster session was invaluable; every conversation sharpened how I think about the problem.

One of the highlights was reuniting with my Robotics Team friends along the way. 🤩 I was so proud to see them receive their awards. Huge congratulations to you all! 🥳

Beyond the conference, I loved exploring the beautiful campus of Zhejiang University (ZJU) and strolling around West Lake (西湖). And yes, I stood on a robot dog while visiting Unitree. 😎😝

Keep moving on~ 💪
