My very first conference, and it happened to be in Singapore. I met so many nice people there, and the banquet even had a dragon dance performance. A perfect welcome for a first-timer. 🐉

Beyond the conference, I explored some of Singapore's most famous spots: the Merlion, Gardens by the Bay, and a visit to NUS. I also dropped by the Standard Chartered Singapore Marathon (SCSM) 2024 to cheer on the marathon runners 🏃‍♀️. And I couldn't leave without visiting the Ghibli exhibition, which was every bit as magical as I'd hoped.

Keep moving on~ 💪
