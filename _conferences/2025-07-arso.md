Finally, not traveling alone. 🥹 There are actually people willing to go crazy with me.🤪 I met some truly wonderful people at the conference and we enjoyed a string duet at the banquet, then went out for the famously good smoothie together. Thank you all~

Beyond the conference, my friends recommended I try the **oiran** photoshoot experience. The costumes and makeup were absolutely stunning. And then, of course, the food. Good food makes me so, so, so happy. 🤤 I could eat a hundred more meals like this and never get tired of it. 😋

Keep moving on~ 💪
